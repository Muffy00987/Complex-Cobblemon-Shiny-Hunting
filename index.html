✨ Shiny Hunting Guide

Use filters to narrow the Pokédex by biome, type, rarity, egg group, or forms. Results stay paginated so changing filters does not force the browser to load hundreds of Pokémon images at once.

Balanced: Starf Berry + Golden Carrot + a matching type/egg-group seasoning.

Maximum shiny: Enchanted Golden Apple + Starf Berry + a target seasoning. Enchanted Golden Apples are powerful but expensive, so Starf Berries remain the practical renewable option.

Targeting: Type and egg-group seasonings increase the matching spawn weight and can be stacked.

Complex can customize spawn rules and rates server-side. Treat this as a hunting reference and verify unusual server-specific behavior in game.

ℹ About & Disclaimer

This free reference is focused on the essentials: searchable spawn data, biome hunting, forms/variants, encounter details, checklist support, and Poké Snack / Poké Bait planning.

The site reads data/spawn-data.js, which is refreshed automatically from public Cobblemon/PokéAPI sources. Complex Cobblemon may have custom server-side spawns, rarities, disabled species, or private datapacks that public data cannot see.
